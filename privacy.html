Privacy

Last updated November 3, 2026

Your audit stays on your device

The planner reads your audit in your browser. Your audit, grades, and plan are not sent to MyDegreePal or anyone else.

While your tab is open, the classes, grades, GPA, and requirements read from your audit are kept in your browser’s session storage, along with your settings, so a refresh doesn’t lose your plan. Closing the tab or selecting Start over clears them. The audit file you saved stays on your device until you delete it.

No accounts

You don’t need to sign in, and this version doesn’t collect your name or email. To keep a plan, use Print or save PDF. The PDF is created by your browser and stays with you.

If you had an account on the earlier version

Earlier versions of MyDegreePal offered Google sign-in, saved plans, and PDF copies stored in your account. This version doesn’t connect to those records. To get a copy of your saved plans or delete your account data, email the address below.

No cookies or analytics

This version doesn’t set cookies and doesn’t use analytics or advertising tools.

Hosting

Vercel hosts the website. Like any website host, it processes basic technical request information, such as IP addresses, to deliver pages. Your audit contents aren’t part of those requests.

Who runs MyDegreePal

MyDegreePal is an independent student project operated by Dawood Khan. It isn’t affiliated with UC Merced and doesn’t replace official advising or registration.

For privacy questions or requests about your account data, contact wheredawoodat949@gmail.com.

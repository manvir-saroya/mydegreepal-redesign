## Summary

Spring 2027 classes start Tuesday, January 19, 2027 and end Friday, May 7, with finals May 8-14. The last day to add or drop is February 8 and the last day to withdraw (with instructor and Dean permission) is April 6. Continuing students register for Spring 2027 from November 2-13, 2026, at an appointment set by their completed and in-progress units: seniors first, freshmen last. New first-year and transfer students register on December 10, 2026, and continuing-student registration pauses from December 7 (11:59 pm) to December 11 (9 am). Summer 2027 runs in sessions from May 24 to August 13, with registration February 22-26. Fall 2027 instruction runs August 25 - December 10, 2027, with registration April 5-16, 2027.

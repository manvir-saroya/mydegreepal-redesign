# UC Merced official policies (for MyDegreePal)

MyDegreePal is an independent student project, not affiliated with UC Merced. These notes quote official UC Merced sources: the 2026-2027 General Catalog (catalog.ucmerced.edu, `catoid=26`), the Office of the Registrar, the Merced Division Senate Regulations, and the school advising offices. They were accessed on 2026-11-03. No third-party sources were used.

Note: catalog.ucmerced.edu sits behind a bot challenge (AWS WAF), so plain `curl` and WebFetch get an empty page. The catalog text was read in a real browser and saved as text snapshots in `sources/`. No official PDF was offered for the calendar or these catalog sections, so no PDFs were downloaded. The 2018 Senate Regulations PDF is superseded by https://senate.ucmerced.edu/node/991, and only that page was used.

| Class level | Freshman 0-29.9, Sophomore 30-59.9, Junior 60-89.9, Senior 90+ completed units | [Catalog 26-27 Enrollment](https://catalog.ucmerced.edu/content.php?catoid=26&navoid=3600) | yes |

| GE structure | LD common (Spark, Written Comm., Quant. Reasoning, Language); UD common (Crossroads, Writing in the Discipline, Culminating Experience); Approaches to Knowledge (2 Area A + 3 Area B); 5 Intellectual Experience badges. Letter grade, C- or better. | Catalog 26-27 GE | yes |
| Credit-earning grades | A, B, C, D (with +/-) and P; not F, NP; I and IP not until replaced | [Catalog 26-27 Grades](https://catalog.ucmerced.edu/content.php?catoid=26&navoid=3601) | yes |
| Incomplete deadline | Fall I: by 1st day of next fall. Spring or summer I: by 1st day of next spring. Otherwise it becomes F/NP. | Catalog 26-27 Grades | yes |
| NR | "No report"; not counted | Catalog 26-27 Grades | yes (no deadline found) |
| W | After week 3 to end of week 10 (with approval); no credit; not counted as attempted | Catalog 26-27 Enrollment | yes |
| P/NP limits | 1 course per term (2 if none the prior term); one-third of UCM units passed; P = C- or better; needs 12+ units enrolled | Catalog 26-27 Grades | yes |
| Repeat policy | Only D, F, NP (WRI 001 also C-). 2nd repeat needs Dean. Grade replacement for first 16 units. Credit given once. | Catalog 26-27 Enrollment | yes |

## Official text

Catalog 2026-27, Enrollment, "Registration Priority":

> "Access to registration (via MyRegistration) is by priority groups. The groups are established according to student class level as determined by the number of completed and in-progress units, with graduate students and seniors registering first, juniors second, etc."

> "The number of semester units a student has completed determines undergraduate classification:"

| Class Level | Units |
|---|---|
| Freshman | 0.0-29.9 |
| Sophomore | 30.0-59.9 |
| Junior | 60.0-89.9 |
| Senior | 90.0 or more |

Registrar, Registration Access Periods:

> "Registration appointments are assigned based on the amount of earned and in-progress units a student has at that time."

The same page's registration-order grid lists "Seniors (90+)", "Juniors (60+)", "Sophmores (30+)" [sic] and "Frosh".

Catalog 2026-27, Enrollment, "Declaration and Change of Major":

> "Except under unusual circumstances, no change of major/concentration/minor will be permitted after a student has attained senior standing (90 units)."

> "Since students are expected to declare into a major by the time they have completed 60 units, the lower division major requirements should be planned into the student's program for the first two years."

## Summary

UC Merced sets class level by completed semester units: Freshman 0-29.9, Sophomore 30-59.9, Junior 60-89.9, Senior 90 or more. For registration priority (the order of enrollment appointments), the Registrar counts completed plus in-progress units when it assigns the appointment, and it does not change an appointment once assigned. Two milestones hang off these numbers: students are expected to declare a major by 60 units, and changing major or minor after reaching 90 units (senior standing) is normally not allowed.

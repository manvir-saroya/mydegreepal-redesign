 COURSE DETAILS 
 For your UC Merced degree, you will take both lower division (lower level) and upper division (upper level) courses.
 What's the difference?
 All courses range from 1-5 units with most courses at UC Merced being 4-units.
 Every semester it is recommended for you register for 12 or more to meet full-time status and 14-16 units to stay on track for normal progress .
 NOTE : Waitlisted units do NOT count toward full-time status.

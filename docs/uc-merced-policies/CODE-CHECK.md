## Mismatches

No hard mismatches with official values. One possible edge-case mismatch: #13, WRI 001 needs a C (not a C-) to satisfy ELWR.

## Gaps worth considering (not covered)

- No warning when a plan runs past the 9th Fall/Spring semester (#7b).
- Transfer students get a first-year 8-semester target (#7a). No official transfer normative time exists to replace it.
- Natural Sciences 150-unit ceiling (#2).
- The notice could add "continuing students" and the December 10, 2026 date for new students (#16).
- Any 19-20 unit option would need a petition label (#3).

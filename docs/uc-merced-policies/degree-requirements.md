# Degree requirements: units, residence, unit caps, university requirements, general education

Accessed 2026-11-03. Catalog year 2026-2027.

## 1. Units for the bachelor's degree: 120 minimum

**Catalog 2026-27, Graduation, "Degree Check"** https://catalog.ucmerced.edu/content.php?catoid=26&navoid=3603

> "The Office of the Registrar will check all pertinent records to ensure that the student has completed a minimum of 120 units and appropriate institutional requirements. The student's School will check for the fulfillment of major and general education requirements."

**Catalog 2026-27, Progress to Degree** (Senate Regulation Part II, Section 1) https://catalog.ucmerced.edu/content.php?catoid=26&navoid=3602

> "... undergraduate students are expected to enroll in and pass an average of 15 units per semester, completing the 120 units necessary for graduation in four years."

## 2. Upper-division unit minimum: NOT FOUND (campus-wide)

The 2026-27 catalog's Academic Policies, the school requirement pages (SSHA, Natural Sciences, Engineering) and the Merced Division Senate Regulations (https://senate.ucmerced.edu/node/991) set no campus-wide minimum number of upper-division units for the bachelor's degree. Upper-division requirements are set by each major. The only campus-wide upper-division unit rule found is a cap on sharing between majors and minors:

> "No more than 12 upper-division units (excluding units required for School and university-level general education) may be used to satisfy requirements for all majors simultaneously ..." (Catalog 2026-27, Enrollment, "Multiple Majors")

## 3. Senior residence requirement: 24 of the last 30 units

**Catalog 2026-27, Graduation, "Residency Requirement"** (Senate Regulation Part II, Section 2. A.)

> "A minimum of 24 of the last 30 units in academic residence is required prior to the award of the bachelor's degree. Under certain circumstances, the appropriate dean or Vice Provost for Undergraduate Education may grant exceptions, such as when a student attends classes at another UC campus as an approved visitor or participates in one of the following: UC Education Abroad, UC Washington Center Program or UC Sacramento Center."

The Senate Regulations page (https://senate.ucmerced.edu/node/991) has the same "24 of the last 30 units" wording.

**Inconsistency in the catalog:** the Enrollment page's "International Opportunities Programs (IOP) Leave" paragraph (https://catalog.ucmerced.edu/content.php?catoid=26&navoid=3600) says instead: "you must comply with the senior Residency Requirement that states that 24 of your last 36 units must be completed on the UC Merced campus. Your last two semesters before graduation cannot be spent studying on a non-UC program." The Senate regulation (24 of the last 30) is the controlling text.

## 4. Scholarship (GPA) requirement: 2.0

**Catalog 2026-27, Graduation, "Scholarship Requirement"** (Senate Regulation Part II, Section 2. B.)

> "To receive a bachelor's degree, a candidate must have a cumulative 2.0 grade point average for all courses attempted in the University."

## 5. Maximum-units caps

- **Campus-wide total-unit cap: NOT FOUND.** No campus-wide maximum on total units, and no "excess units" cap on graduation, appears in the 2026-27 catalog policies or the Senate Regulations. The Minors policy refers to one without saying what it is: "Work for the minor must be completed within the maximum number of units allowed by the student's School."
- **School of Natural Sciences: 150 units.** Catalog 2026-27, School of Natural Sciences (https://catalog.ucmerced.edu/content.php?catoid=26&navoid=3651): "The School of Natural Sciences degree requirements are at least 120, but not more than 150 semester units."
- **School of Engineering and SSHA total-unit cap: NOT FOUND** on their 2026-27 catalog school pages.
- The time limit (9 semesters) is covered in `time-to-degree.md`.

## 6. University of California requirements

**Catalog 2026-27, General Education** https://catalog.ucmerced.edu/content.php?catoid=26&navoid=3664

**Entry Level Writing Requirement (ELWR):**

> "Students must satisfy the ELWR requirement by the end of the second semester of enrollment at UC Merced. Failure to complete this requirement in the time allowed will result in a hold on a student's registration."

It can be met by exam scores (for example "3 or above on the College Board Advanced Placement Examination in English Language and Composition"), "through course selection after completing the Writing Readiness Review (WRR)", or by "completion of WRI 001 at UC Merced with a grade of C or higher."

**American History and Institutions (AH&I):**

> "As a candidate for an undergraduate degree at UC Merced, you need to demonstrate knowledge of American history and of the principles of American institutions under the federal and state constitutions."

It can be met by high school U.S. history or government with grades of C or better, AP U.S. History 3/4/5, SAT II U.S. History 550+, acceptable college coursework, or one listed UC Merced course (CCST 060, CRES 001, CRES 123, HIST 016, HIST 017, HIST 123, HIST 124B, HIST 133, POLI 001, POLI 110, POLI 111).

## 7. UC Merced General Education: structure

Same page. GE has no separate framework brand name in the 2026-27 catalog. It is called "General Education Requirements" and has these parts:

> "Courses must be taken for a letter grade and may not be taken on a pass/no pass basis unless the course is only offered on a pass/no pass basis. You must complete all courses with a C- or better."

- **Lower Division Common Course Requirements:**
  - I. Spark Seminar (SPRK 001/010; "must be completed with a C- or better, prior to the start of the third regular (Fall/Spring) semester")
  - II. Written Communication (WRI 010)
  - III. Quantitative Reasoning
  - IV. Language (coursework, Education Abroad, campus test, AP/IB, high school third year, or State Seal of Biliteracy)
- **Upper Division Common Course Requirements:**
  - I. Crossroads Course
  - II. Writing in the Discipline
  - III. Culminating Experience
- **Approaches to Knowledge:** five courses in all.
  - Area A, Life and Physical Science: one Life Science and one Physical Science.
  - Area B: one Social Science, plus two from Literary and Textual Analysis, Media and Visual Analysis, and Societies and Cultures of the Past.
- **Intellectual Experiences (badges):** Scientific Method, Diversity and Identity, Global Awareness, Sustainability, Ethics. "Courses can be used to satisfy more than one Intellectual Experiences Badge, but not more than two."
- **Sharing rule:** "With the exception of Spark Seminar, lower and upper division common course requirements may be shared with your major, concentration, or minor requirements."

## Summary

A UC Merced bachelor's degree needs at least 120 semester units and a cumulative UC GPA of 2.0. At least 24 of the last 30 units must be taken in residence; one catalog paragraph about non-UC study abroad says "24 of your last 36", which conflicts with the Senate regulation. The degree also needs the two UC-wide requirements (Entry Level Writing, due by the end of the second semester, and American History and Institutions), UC Merced GE, and the major. GE is made up of lower-division common courses (Spark, Writing, Quantitative Reasoning, Language), upper-division common courses (Crossroads, Writing in the Discipline, Culminating Experience), five Approaches to Knowledge courses (2 in Area A, 3 in Area B) and five Intellectual Experience badges. All GE courses must be taken for a letter grade with a C- or better. There is no campus-wide upper-division unit minimum and no campus-wide total-unit cap. Natural Sciences caps degrees at 150 units.

# Normative time to degree

Accessed 2026-11-03.

## Campus policy: 8 semesters (4 years); approval needed beyond 9

**Catalog 2026-27, Progress to Degree and Academic Standing, "Undergraduate Normal Progress to Degree"** (Senate Regulation Part II, Section 1)
https://catalog.ucmerced.edu/content.php?catoid=26&navoid=3602

> "UC Merced undergraduate degree programs are designed to be completed in eight semesters or four academic years. To meet the normal progress requirement, undergraduate students are expected to enroll in and pass an average of 15 units per semester, completing the 120 units necessary for graduation in four years. The Registrar's Office and the appropriate Dean will ensure that students are making normal progress towards their degrees. Extensions of enrollment beyond 9 terms require the approval of the student's School."

The Merced Division Senate Regulations (https://senate.ucmerced.edu/node/991) say the same ("designed to be completed in 8 terms or 4 academic years").

## School rules

**School of Natural Sciences** (https://ns-advising.ucmerced.edu/policies/ns-academic-policies/normal-progress-degree):
> students must complete degree requirements "within 9 semesters (not counting summer sessions)". "Enrollment beyond 9 semesters requires a petition to be reviewed by the School Dean." "Extensions of enrollment will not be approved to complete a 2nd major or minor ..."

**School of Engineering** (https://engr-advising.ucmerced.edu/policies/normal-progress):
> "The School of Engineering will automatically grant approval for students to continue enrollment to 10 semesters, if needed." A student declaring after the fourth semester must show they can finish "within 10 total semesters (not including summers)."

## Transfer students: NOT FOUND as a policy

No official UC Merced page found states a normative time to degree for junior transfers (for example "4 semesters" or "2 years"). The only figure found is descriptive. Admissions, Transfer FAQs (https://admissions.ucmerced.edu/transfer/faqs): "At UC Merced our transfer students typically graduate in 2.4 years (18-19 data)."

## Summary

UC Merced designs every bachelor's program to take eight Fall/Spring semesters (four years) at an average of 15 units a semester. Summers do not count as semesters. Going past nine semesters needs your School's approval. Natural Sciences enforces 9 semesters strictly and grants extensions rarely, while Engineering automatically allows up to 10. No official normative time for transfer students was found. Admissions reports only that transfers typically graduate in 2.4 years.

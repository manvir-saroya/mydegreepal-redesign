# Unit limits per semester, full-time, minimum progress, summer

Accessed 2026-11-03. Catalog year 2026-2027 where noted. The advising and summer pages show no date beyond the "© 2026" footer.

## 1. Maximum units per semester without approval: 18

No campus-wide maximum appears in the 2026-27 catalog's Academic Policies or in the Merced Division Senate Regulations. The limit comes from UC Merced advising offices. All of them use 18 as the most a student can take without approval.

**Bobcat Advising Center (first-year advising), "Understanding Courses and Units"**
https://bobcat-advising-center.ucmerced.edu/registration/registration/understanding-courses-and-units
The "MAXIMUM UNITS PER SEMESTERS" section is an image (saved as `sources/bobcat-advising_max-units-per-semester.png`). It reads:

> "First semester: 18 units maximum, NO exception"
> "Second semester: 18 units maximum, 19+ units with petition and good GPA"

**School of Engineering, "School of Engineering Policies"**
https://engr-advising.ucmerced.edu/policies/soe-policies

> "Students must apply for permission to enroll in excess units each semester. Approval to take more than 18 units is conditional and determined by your academic advisor."

> "Students must have a previous term GPA and a cumulative GPA of 3.0 or higher" (first-year GPA thresholds may be higher)

**School of Social Sciences, Humanities and Arts (SSHA), "Registration"**
https://ssha-advising.ucmerced.edu/registration

> "SSHA Policy states that students requesting to enroll in more than 18 units during a semester must have a 3.0 overall GPA or a 3.3 GPA in the most recent term if the overall GPA does not meet the standard."

> "Enrollment in more than 20 units will require approval by the Dean."

(Students who qualify submit the "Petition for Excess Units" webform.)

**School of Natural Sciences, Advising FAQ**
https://ns-advising.ucmerced.edu/resources/faq

> "Students must have a cumulative GPA of 3.0 or higher to be approved for enrollment in more than 18 units in a single semester. In order to do so, you must email your advisor for permission."

## 2. Full-time minimum and normal load

**Catalog 2026-27, Enrollment, "Enrollment Status"** https://catalog.ucmerced.edu/content.php?catoid=26&navoid=3600

> "Undergraduate students must carry a study load of at least 15 units each semester in order to maintain normal progress toward their degree. At least 12 units are required for undergraduates to be certified as full-time students for financial aid purposes and to meet minimum progress requirements."

> Part-time: "undergraduate students must be registered for 10 units or fewer by the 15th day of instruction" and "Minimum progress requirements are waived for approved part-time students."

## 3. Minimum progress

**Catalog 2026-27, Progress to Degree and Academic Standing, "C. Minimum Progress"** (Senate Regulation Part II, Section 3. C.) https://catalog.ucmerced.edu/content.php?catoid=26&navoid=3602

> "An undergraduate student is subject to administrative probation if the student does not complete a minimum of 24 University of California units during an academic year, including summer."

The School of Engineering adds its own normal-progress rule (catalog, School of Engineering page https://catalog.ucmerced.edu/content.php?catoid=26&navoid=3652):

> "Each student must register in at least 12 units per semester ... Each student must successfully complete — at UC Merced — no fewer than the following number of units: 15 x (N-1) = units needed. N = semesters at UC Merced. Summer sessions are not counted as semesters."

## 4. Summer unit limit

**UC Merced Summer Session FAQ** https://summersession.ucmerced.edu/contact-faq/faq

> "Students are advised to take a maximum of 12 units during Summer Session. Units above the maximum are allowed only with the permission of a student's academic advisor."

> "You must complete a minimum of six (6) semester units for Summer Session financial aid."

## 5. P/NP eligibility tied to load

**Catalog 2026-27, Grades, "Grade Passed/Not Passed"** https://catalog.ucmerced.edu/content.php?catoid=26&navoid=3601

> "Undergraduate students in good standing who are enrolled in at least 12 units may take certain courses on a Passed/Not Passed (P/NP) basis."

## Summary

You can register for up to 18 units a semester without asking anyone. In your first semester, 18 is a hard cap. From the second semester on, each school lets you go above 18 by petition if your GPA is good enough: Engineering and Natural Sciences want 3.0, SSHA wants 3.0 overall (or 3.3 last term), and SSHA needs the Dean's approval above 20. Full-time status starts at 12 units. Normal progress expects 15 units a semester (30 a year), and completing fewer than 24 UC units in an academic year (summer included) puts you on administrative probation. Summer Session advises at most 12 units, and more needs your advisor's permission. NOT FOUND: a single campus-wide maximum written into the catalog or the Senate regulations.
